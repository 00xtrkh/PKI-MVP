Certificate Signing Requests

Build the key and CSR on your machine with OpenSSL. Upload only the CSR. Never upload your private key. Issuing is admin-only.

Create a CSR with OpenSSL
  1. Replace Alice with your Common Name.
  2. Keep alice.key on your machine only.
  3. Upload alice.csr (or paste the PEM below).
openssl genrsa -out alice.key {{ client_key_size }}

openssl req -new -key alice.key -out alice.csr \
  -subj "/C={{ ca_country }}/O={{ client_organization }}/CN=Alice"

openssl req -in alice.csr -noout -text -verify
